## Round-1 Blocker Recheck

- **Not resolved: [COMPLETENESS_GAP] Neither new test can compile where the plan puts it.** The prescribed fix — `#[cfg(test)] pub(super) use planning::involved_table_columns;` in `joins/mod.rs` — does not compile, for two independent reasons, both reproduced with `rustc` against faithful module trees. (1) `E0252`: `joins/mod.rs:28` already binds that exact name in the same module (`use planning::{involved_table_columns, join_requires_exasol_postprocessing, resolve_one_join_side, select_broadcast_sides};`), and a second `use` of the same name in one module is "the name `involved_table_columns` is defined multiple times" even when both resolve to the same item. (2) `E0364`: `involved_table_columns` is declared `pub(super)` at `joins/planning.rs:359`, i.e. `pub(in adapter::pushdown::joins)`; a `pub(super) use` in `joins/mod.rs` re-exports at `pub(in adapter::pushdown)`, which is broader than the item's own visibility — "`involved_table_columns` is private, and cannot be re-exported". The precedent block the fix mirrors is not a counterexample: its two items are declared `pub(in super::super)` (`sql_builders.rs:716`, `sql_builders.rs:777`), not `pub(super)`, which is exactly why re-exporting them at `pub(super)` is legal. See finding 1 below for the verified working shape.
- **Resolved: [UNSTATED_ASSUMPTION] The delta recorded a divergence nobody measured.** The rescope is correct and the arithmetic checks out under Rust's real `char` case tables (compiled and run): `"straße".to_uppercase()` = `STRASSE`, `"STRAßE".to_uppercase()` = `STRASSE`, `"STRAßE".to_ascii_uppercase()` = `STRAßE` unchanged — so the two folds do diverge on the delivered form. The counterexample also holds: `"nüm".to_uppercase()` = `NÜM` and both folds are no-ops on `NÜM`, so the original example was indeed unreachable. Task 3 exists, is stated as a halting gate, and the renumbering (old 3→4, 4→5, 5→6) is consistent across § Design → Patterns, § Non-Goals, § Impact, § Parallelization, § Verification, and both delta scenarios. Two residuals are raised as advisories, not as a re-blocker: the composition's final link is still inferred (finding 2) and decision-log decision [6] kept the pre-renumber task numbers (finding 10).
- **Resolved: [REQUIREMENT_CONFLICT] The row-3 gate contradicted the doc-comment preservation clause.** Ran the gate's own grep against the current tree: five hits, all in `support.rs` — `674`, `811` (the doc comment), `828`, `1013`, `1015`. Task 2 removes the four code hits and now instructs the `811` reword; delta line 102 excepts a sentence naming a predicate that moves to `classify_exa_type` while still requiring the integer-`DECIMAL(p,0)` rationale be kept. The gate flips from 5 hits to 0 and is satisfiable. Two weaknesses in the same row are new advisories (finding 7).
