## Feasibility

#### [NFR_IGNORED] ADVISORY
- Location: plan.md § Impact item 3 ("No migration, no breaking change"), § Migration row 1
- Issue: branch (a) makes the note removal one-way, and no artifact states the rollback consequence. After an operator deploys the new `.so` and runs `REFRESH`, the persisted `CLUSTER_NODES` is gone. If the operator then rolls the `.so` back, the pre-refactor `handle_pushdown_request` reads an absent note and falls to `unwrap_or(1)` (`adapter/mod.rs:373-376`), so `G` collapses from `node_count × parallelism_factor` to `1 × parallelism_factor` on every pushdown until the operator runs another `REFRESH` to re-write the note. The degradation is silent: query results stay correct and only the fan-out shrinks, which is exactly the failure mode plan.md § Design already flags the `0 => 1` floor for. Under the rejected branch (b) a rollback was inert, so this asymmetry is introduced by the round-2 fix and is currently unrecorded.
- Fix: Add a row to plan.md § Migration reading "Rollback to a pre-refactor `.so` after a `REFRESH` | The old adapter reads no `CLUSTER_NODES` and shards as `1 × PARALLELISM_FACTOR` until an `ALTER VIRTUAL SCHEMA ... REFRESH` re-writes the note; results are unaffected, fan-out shrinks", and delete "no breaking change" from § Impact item 3's heading so the item's title matches its own body.
