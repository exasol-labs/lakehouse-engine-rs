[1m== Reading[0m
   ADDITIONAL_ISSUERS widens the accepted iss values of one authenticator; it
   does not add a key. build_oidc_authenticator fetches the JWKS once from
   provider.uri (JWKSWebAuthenticator::new) and then calls
   add_additional_issuers(...) on that same authenticator, so a token signed
   by the engine has no matching key and fails signature validation.
   A dedicated provider entry is therefore the only way in, and that is what
   puts engine principals under the engine~ prefix.
