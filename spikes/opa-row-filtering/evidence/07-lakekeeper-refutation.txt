CONCLUSION: the Lakekeeper bridge maps Trino operations onto Lakekeeper
CATALOG permissions via /management/v1/action/batch-check and answers with a
boolean 'allow'. SelectFromColumns / FilterColumns give column-level ALLOW-DENY,
which is access, not masking. No row filter, no column mask, anywhere.
